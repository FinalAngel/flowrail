# Local-first software, in short

Our own summary of the idea Paper Plane is built on.

- The copy on your disk is the real one. The server is a relay, not the owner.
- Offline is the normal case, not an error state.
- Two devices will disagree; the app has to merge, not pick a winner.

See `notes.md` for the questions this raised for our sync design.
