# Support

One file, three things a reader can ask for. Each action has its own steps.

## Action: Answer a question

### Step 1: Find the answer in docs/

Quote the paragraph that answers it. If the docs do not answer it, say so and file a docs task.

### Step 2: Draft the reply

A draft only. Short, friendly, with the link to the paragraph.

## Action: Refund a payment

### Step 1: Check the payment

Find it by the reader's email. Note the date and the amount.

### Step 2: SIGN-OFF on the refund

Stop. The human refunds or says no. The agent never moves money.

## Action: Close an account

### Step 1: Export the reader's notes

Zip them and attach the zip to the draft reply.

### Step 2: MANDATORY check with the maintainer

Closing an account deletes the sync store. Nothing happens without a yes.
